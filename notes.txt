npm install stockfish


    Stockfish evaluates the position
    You send your board (FEN) to Stockfish (local engine or WASM build).
    Stockfish gives you:
        best move
        top 3 moves
        eval score
        depth
        tactics flagged by the engine
        if any piece is hanging
        if there is a forced tactic
        recommended captures
        if your last move was a blunder/inaccuracy/book move
        full PV (principal variation)


    GPT is amazing at explanation
    GPT is terrible at tactical calculation
    Stockfish is amazing at tactics
    Stockfish is terrible at human explanation
